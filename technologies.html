    How We Build

    Our Development Process

    A structured, agile methodology that keeps you informed at every stage and delivers quality without surprises.

    01

    Discovery & Architecture

    We analyse your requirements, define the technical architecture, select the right stack, and set up the project infrastructure with CI/CD from day one.

    02

    Design System & Prototyping

    We build a component library and interactive Figma prototype before writing a single line of production code — saving time and aligning stakeholders early.

    03

    Agile Development Sprints

    Two-week sprints with daily standups, weekly demos, and a transparent Jira board mean you always know exactly what's being built and when it ships.

    04

    QA, Testing & Performance Audit

    Comprehensive unit, integration, and end-to-end testing. Lighthouse audits target 90+ scores for Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO.

    05

    Deployment & Go-Live

    Zero-downtime deployments via automated pipelines to your infrastructure of choice — AWS, GCP, Vercel, or on-premise. SSL, CDN, and monitoring included.

    06

    Ongoing Support & Iteration

    Post-launch retainer plans cover monitoring, security patches, feature iterations, and performance optimisation so your product stays best-in-class.
