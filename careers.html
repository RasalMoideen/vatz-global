    Life at VATZ

    Where Great Work Feels Like Play

    At VATZ, we believe the best creative work happens when talented people feel trusted, energised, and genuinely excited about what they're building. We've deliberately built a culture that removes bureaucracy, rewards bold ideas, and invests deeply in every person on the team.

    Our Process

    How We Hire

    Our process is designed to be fair, fast, and respectful of your time. Most candidates go from first contact to offer in under three weeks.

    01

    Application Review

    Submit your application via the role links above. We review every application personally within 5 business days and provide a clear yes or no — no ghosting, ever.

    02

    Intro Call (30 min)

    A relaxed conversation with our talent team. We'll walk you through VATZ, the role, and our culture — and we want to hear about you, your goals, and what great work means to you.

    03

    Skills Assessment

    A focused, take-home challenge scoped to around 3 hours. We design these to mirror real work — no abstract puzzles or whiteboard coding. Your time is respected and compensated for senior roles.

    04

    Team Interview (60 min)

    Meet 2–3 people from the team you'd be joining. This is a two-way conversation — ask us anything. We explore your thinking process, how you collaborate, and walk through your assessment together.

    05

    Founder / Leadership Chat (30 min)

    For senior and leadership roles, a final conversation with Alex Rivera (Creative Director) or a department head. Focused on vision, alignment, and long-term growth ambitions.

    06

    Offer & Onboarding

    Receive a clear, written offer within 48 hours of your final interview. Our onboarding program is 4 weeks of structured ramp-up so you're set up for success from day one.
