    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does a typical project take?

    Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A brand identity typically takes 3–5 weeks, a website 6–10 weeks, and a full campaign 8–16 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline in your proposal.

    What is your design process?

    We follow a four-phase approach: Discovery & Research → Strategy & Planning → Design & Development → Launch & Optimize. You'll be involved at every stage with regular check-ins and feedback loops.

    Do you work with international clients?

    Absolutely. We work with clients across the globe. Our team is distributed across multiple time zones to ensure seamless communication regardless of your location.

    What happens after the project is launched?

    All projects include a 30-day post-launch support period. We also offer ongoing retainer packages for maintenance, content updates, marketing, and continuous optimization.
